# National AI Olympiad: K study three: the crop doctor AI (from part two)
**The scenario:** startup called farmGuard wants to help farmers protect their crops. Their idea is to build a mobile app where a farmer take a picture of a plant leaf and an AI will instantly diagnose if it has a disease.

**The challenge:** to do this they need to build a powerful deep learning neural network.

**AI concepts in action:**
- **neural networks (parentheses chapter 5):** from God's AI is built on a neural network. The input layer takes the pixels from the photo the hidden layers learn to recognizing simple patterns like leaf edges, then more complex patterns like spots or discoloration and finally, the specific visual signs of a disease like tomato light.
- **data is King (chapter 6):** the AI is useless without data. The fun team spent months creating a massive data set. They took over hundred thousand pictures of healthy and deceased plans.
- **cleaning and preparing data (chapter six):** the raw photos are enough a team of data scientist had to clean the data. They removed blurry photos cropped image to focus on the leaves and carefully labeled each picture with the correct disease name this high-quality labeled data set was ascension for training and accurate AI.

**The result:** after training on the clean data set the FarmGuard app could identify plant diseases with 99% accuracy, helping farmers save the crops and prevent food shortages
