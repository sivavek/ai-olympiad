# National AI Olympia: case study six: the delivery drone challenge (from part four)
**The Scenario:** an Olympiad contestant is given a problem from logistics company QuickFly that uses drones for delivery. The company needs to find the most efficient route for drone to deliver packages to three different locations before return returning to the warehouse.

**The Challenge:** the contestant must design an algorithm to find the shortest possible route the optimal path. This is a classic problem and computer science known as the traveling sales person problem.
## AI concepts in action.
- **algorithmic thinking (chapter 10):** the contestant can't just guess, they need to think like a computer and create a step-by-step plan.
- **optimization:** the goal is not just to find a path but to find the best path that saves the most time and battery life.
- **logic and problem-solving:** the contestant might first try a simple greedy algorithm, always fly to the closest next location they would test this logic and see if it works. they might find that this doesn't always give the shortest overall route.
- **a better algorithm:** a more advanced approach would be to calculate the total distance for every possible combination of routes (eg, A>B>C>D vs A>C>B>D) and then choose the shortest one.

**The solution:** by applying logical reasoning, and algorithmic, thinking that contestant can design and test solution that provides a drone with the most efficient flightpath, demonstrating the kind of problem-solving skills needed to excel in the Olympia
