National AI Olympiad: Biased Hiring Bot (From Part 1)
=====================================================

**The Scenario:** A fast-growing tech company, "Innovate Inc." decide to use AI to help them sort through thousands of job applications. They trained their AI model on the company's hiring data from the past 10 years hoping it would learn to pick the best candidates.

**The Problem:** After a year, the company noticed a strange pattern. The AI was almost exclusively. Recommending male candidates for engineering roles, and female candidates for marketing roles. Talented applicants are being unfairly rejected

- **AI concept in action (chapter 3):** The AI wasn't programmed to be biased. It learned a bias from the data it was given. The company's hiring history was unintentionally biased. They had more men for engineering roles in the past, and the AI learned this as a rule engineers are usually men.
- **Garbage in and garbage out (Chapter 6):** The historical data, while accurate, was not representative of a fair hiring process. By feeding the AI, this biased data the company got biased results.

**The solution:** Innovate Inc. had to retrain their AI. They created a new balanced data set that included an equal number of successful, male and female employees in every role. They also added rules to ensure the AI ignored personal information like gender and focused only skills and experience.
