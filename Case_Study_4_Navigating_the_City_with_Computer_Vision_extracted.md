# National AI Olympia: case study four: navigating the city with Computer mission (from three).
**The scenario:** a self driving car the Voyager is on admission to drive across a PCCC to do this. It needs to see and understand the world around it in real time.
**The technology:** the Voyager is acute with multiple cameras that constantly feed video to it on board AI brain.
## AI concepts in action:
- **computer vision  (chapter 7):** this is the core technology. The AI uses computer mission to interpret the video feeds. It doesn't just see a red shape. It identifies as a stop sign.
- **image classification:** the AI classifies thousands of objects per second. It identifies pedestrians bicycles, other cars, traffic lights, and lane lines.
- **pattern recognition:** through training, the AI recognizing patterns that predict behaviour. For example, a recognizing that a blinking turn signal on the car in front means it's about to turn. It sees a giant chasing a ball near the sidewalk and predicts they might run into the street.

**The result:** by constantly, seeing interpreting and predicting the computer vision system allows "the Voyager" to make safe split second driving decisions from stopping at a red light to yeilding for pedestrian.
