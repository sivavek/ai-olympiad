# National AI Olympia: K study five: Spotify mood playlist (three).
**The scenario:** You have had a long day and just want to listen to some relaxing music you open Spotify and see a playlist called "chill hits" how did Spotify know which songs were "chill"?.

**Technology:** Spotify uses natural language processing (NLP) to understand the content and feeling of music.

## AI concepts in action.
- **natural language processing ((NLP) (Chapter 8):** Spotify AI doesn't just look at the genre it analyzes the data surrounding the music.
- **sentiment analysis:** the AI reads millions of block post music, reviews, and social media comments about songs. It uses NLP to understand the sentiment or emotion in the text. If thousands of people describe a strong using words like calm, relaxing, or peaceful, the AI tags that song as chill.
- **Analyzing lyrics:** the NLP modern also analyze the actual lyrics of the songs, songs with slower, tempos and lyrics about love or reflection or more like likely to be classified as than songs with fast tempos and lyrics about party.

**The Result:** By using NLP to understand the language People used to describe music, Spotify can automatically curate playlists that perfectly match a specific mood or activity, creating a deeply personal listening experience.
