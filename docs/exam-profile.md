# Exam profile

## Confirmed from the public TalentSprint FAQ

- The National AI Olympiad has a Foundation track for school students in Grades 6–8 and 9–12.
- Stage 1 is a 50-question, 90-minute online exam.
- The 2026 exam is scheduled for 8–9 August 2026; participants choose an available slot.
- Registered participants receive one or two sample papers through the platform.
- The Prep Kit is an optional paid digital add-on.
- Stage 2 is a hands-on project with mentorship for top Stage 1 rankers.
- The FAQ says Stage 2 is expected in October/November 2026; final instructions should be checked in the registered account and email.

Official source: [TalentSprint National AI Olympiad FAQ](https://talentsprint.com/national-ai-olympiad/faq/)

## Practical interpretation for preparation

The safest preparation scope is:

1. Python fundamentals and code reading;
2. logical and verbal reasoning;
3. AI vocabulary and everyday applications;
4. introductory machine learning, computer vision, and NLP;
5. responsible AI, bias, privacy, and real-world case analysis;
6. timed multiple-choice practice.

This list is a preparation model, not a claim that every item is an official question topic.

## Support and access

Use the registered platform and the official support channel for access problems, sample papers, slot selection, or exam instructions. This repository is a study companion, not a replacement for the participant dashboard.
